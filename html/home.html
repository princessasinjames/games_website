1. Stardew Valley

Stardew Valley is a game where you mainly build your farm and interact with villagers. There is a large a variety of crops, crafts, buildings, and barns to fill your farm with. There are also certain crops that you can plant during the 4 in-game seasons (spring, summer, fall, and winter).

These are the crops per season:
1. Spring: carrots, cauliflower, coffee beans, garlic, green beans, kale, parsnips, potatoes, rhubarb, strawberries, and unmilled rice
2. Summer: blueberries, coffee beans, corn, hops, hot peppers, melons, radishes, red cabbage, starfruit, summer squash, sunflowers, tomatoes, and wheat
3. Fall: amaranth, artichokes, beets, bok choy, broccoli, corn, cranberries, eggplants, grapes, pumpkins, sunflowers, wheat, and yams
4. Winter: powdermelons
5. Special muli-seasonal crops: ancient fruit, cactus fruit, pineapples, Qi fruit, sweet gem berries, taro roots, and tea leaves

Relationships in Stardew Valley are arguably more complicated than those in Animal Crossing (New Horizons). This is because Stardew Valley villagers have more hearts, speciak gifts that either increase, decrease, or have little effect on their affection level, and you can marry some of them in game. These gifts are based on the person, not just type of item & color, as they are in Animal Crossing (New Horizons).

2. Animal Crossing (New Horizons)

Animal Crossing (New Horizons) is a game about befriending animals, exploring temporary islands, and build a base with things that you find or buy. These things can be bought from "Nook's Cranny" (a market that changes its inverntory every day to something else), villagers who randomly sell stuff throughout the game, NPC's from "Harv's Island", and several other sources (like the "Abel Sisters").

Figure 3: free use video from pixabay.

3. Fall Guys

Fall Guys is a game where you mainly compete with other players to reach a finish line. There are different modes of the game such as solo, duos, squads, ranked, and special rounds. Solo games involve joining solo competitions for first place in a series of matches. Duos involves competing in a similar way, but with another person. That person could be a friend or random. Squads is similar, though you can have up to 4 players in a team. Ranked matches can give rewards at the end of the season depending on your rank. Some players display these rewards in game for bragging rights, intimidating, or host of other personal reasons. Special matches occur on unique maps that come and go. There is also a battle pass with free and paid rewards. These rewards are usually cosmetic (like a new skin, emoticon, or phrase).

4. Minecraft

Minecraft is a game where you can try to survive in a world with/without monsters, or build infinitely in a creative world where you cannot die of natural causes. There are many animals in Minecraft which can be helpful for survival, especially if you create a farm. This is an interesting way to survive in the game, since alternatives are fishing after crafting/finding a fishing rod, eating rotten flesh from zombies which only spawn at night, growing crops for days, or trying to find random edible food items (i.e. sweet berries and mushrooms). Building is another part of the game which I like, since there are so many things you can build with and many different kinds of wood, including cherry wood, which is adorably pink.

5. Klondike Solitaire

Klondike Solitaire is nice because you can play it offline or online. It is another way to pass time when bored. It it a sorting game where your goal is to place cards in descending order in four unique staacks. The challenge lies in the rules. Sometimes matches can be impossible to win. Some people even play a version of the game where you can shuffle the hidden cards if you get stuck. In this way, it is a little like Uno, in that people use different rules, with general similarities, sometimes to gain their own favors. Since Klondike Solitaire is mainly a solo game, the presure to win depends on the person playing.

The basic rules of Klondike Solitaire:
1.You can only sort teh cards onto 1 of 4 foundations.
2. you can only place cards of matching suits (aces, hearts, clubs, and spades) in order from the ace to the king.
3. You can only use a card from the top of a stack or the waste pile (moving a card from a stack allows you to reveal the one under it).

Solitaire is a well-known game which has many tutorials online. There are also many apps for solitaire and variations of the game like: spider, pyramid, and freecell solitaire. It kind of reminds me of Wilmot's Warehouse, which I could haev talked about instead. I would asy it is pretty interesting and worth a play, though it can get pretty high-paced.
